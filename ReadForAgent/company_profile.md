# Company Profile: Green Nepal Krishi Farm

## 1. Company Overview
**Name:** Green Nepal Krishi Farm  
**Industry:** Commercial Agriculture / Farming  
**Location:** Nepal  
**Description:** Green Nepal Krishi Farm is a modern, commercial agricultural enterprise dedicated to sustainable farming practices, high-quality crop production, and empowering the local agricultural community. 

## 2. Mission & Vision
*   **Mission:** To cultivate high-quality, sustainable, and fresh agricultural produce while implementing modern farming technologies to enhance food security and support local economies in Nepal.
*   **Vision:** To be the leading commercial agricultural brand in Nepal, recognized for innovation, environmental stewardship, and premium-quality crops.

## 3. Brand Identity
*   **Tone of Voice:** Trustworthy, earthy, professional, and forward-thinking. The language should be accessible but highlight expertise in modern agriculture.
*   **Visual Themes:** Natural greens, earth tones, vibrant crop imagery, and modern, clean layouts (reflecting the use of GSAP and smooth scrolling).
*   **Core Values:** 
    *   Sustainability
    *   Quality & Freshness
    *   Community & Empowerment
    *   Technological Innovation in Agriculture

## 4. Target Audience
*   **B2B (Business to Business):** Local supermarkets, wholesale distributors, restaurants, and food processing companies looking for reliable bulk produce.
*   **B2C (Business to Consumer):** Health-conscious individuals and families seeking fresh, locally sourced farm produce.
*   **Prospective Employees:** Agricultural experts, farm managers, and laborers looking for career opportunities (via the Careers page).
*   **General Public:** People interested in farming news, agricultural education, and sustainable practices (via the Blog/News page).

## 5. Key Offerings (Website Focus)
*   **Produce (Crops):** Detailed information about the seasonal and staple crops grown on the farm.
*   **Careers/Work:** Opportunities to join the Green Nepal Krishi Farm team.
*   **News & Updates:** Insights into farming practices, harvest seasons, and company milestones.

## 6. Contact & Enquiries
*   The business actively encourages inquiries for bulk orders, partnerships, and general questions through the website's contact form, which is managed via the admin dashboard.
